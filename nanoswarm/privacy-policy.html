Swarmling — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 September 2026

Swarmling ("the game") is made by Sanne ("we"). This policy explains what data the game uses, why, and the choices you have. We do not run our own servers and we do not sell your data.

1. Data the game stores on your device

Your colony progress, settings and purchase history are saved only on your device. They are not sent to us. Uninstalling the game deletes them.

2. Data collected by third-party services

The game uses the following Google services. Each one processes data under its own privacy policy.

ServiceWhat it usesWhy
Google AdMob Advertising ID, device and app information, approximate location from IP address, ad interactions To show ads that support the game, measure them and prevent fraud. Where the law requires it (for example in the EEA and UK) you are asked for consent first. You can change your answer at any time in Settings → Privacy options.
Google Analytics for Firebase App-instance ID, device information, in-game events (for example "reached a new level", "opened the shop") To understand how the game is played and improve it. It never includes your name, email or contacts. Only sent with your consent and while Settings → Share usage data is on.
Firebase Crashlytics Crash reports: device model, OS version, app state at the moment of the crash To find and fix crashes. Turned off by Settings → Share usage data.
Firebase Remote Config App-instance ID To adjust game balance without an app update.
Google Play Billing Purchase information handled by Google Play To process in-app purchases. We never see your payment details.

Privacy policies: Google · AdMob · Firebase.

3. Notifications

The game can show local reminders (for example "your colony is ready"). They are scheduled on your device and need no data to be sent. You can turn them off in Settings → Notifications or in Android settings.

4. Your choices

  • Ads consent: Settings → Privacy options (shown where the law requires it).
  • Analytics and crash reports: Settings → Share usage data.
  • Personalised ads: Android Settings → Google → Ads → delete or reset your advertising ID.
  • Delete data: uninstall the game to remove all data on your device. To ask for Analytics or Crashlytics data to be deleted, email us and include the date you installed the game.

5. Children

Swarmling is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them.

6. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
